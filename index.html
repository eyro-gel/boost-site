The two minutes that decide it

Your next customer is deciding right now, and you are not in the room.

They open Maps, type a search and give the results about ten seconds each. Rating, review count, recent photos, whether the booking link works. The whole thing happens before anyone reaches your website — and most operators have never once looked at it from that side.

01 — THEY SEARCH

Intent, with a place attached

"Best spa Seminyak." "Dive shop Koh Tao." "Hotel near Ha Long pier." If you are below the first handful of results, you are not in the shortlist at all.

02 — THEY JUDGE

Four signals, ten seconds

Star rating. How many reviews. Whether the photos look like this year. Whether the hours say open. That is the entire first impression, and none of it is on your website.

03 — THEY BOOK

One tap forward, or one tap back

A working reserve button keeps them. A broken link, a slow page or a form asking for eight fields sends them back to the results — where a competitor is waiting.

About BOOST

Hands-on, specialised and based where you are.

BOOST works with tourism and tourism-dependent businesses across South-East Asia — accommodation, food and drink, wellness, tours, activities, clinics and the businesses that live on visitor traffic. One industry, one region. We are not trying to be a general agency.

The reason is narrow expertise. Search behaviour in a beach town in Krabi is not the same as in Bangkok, the platforms that matter in Vietnam are not the ones that matter in the Philippines, and a dive shop's booking path has almost nothing in common with a spa's. Knowing that is most of the job.

BOOST is new, and we are not going to pretend otherwise. There are no client logos on this page because we have not earned them yet, and we would rather show you nothing than borrow someone else's credibility. The free week exists for exactly this reason: judge us on what we do in your business, not on a page of testimonials.

Anything not answered here, ask us directly — we will give you a straight answer or tell you we do not know.

What happens during the free week?

It opens with a short onboarding call to agree what matters most, then we work: a review of your priority pages, an audit of your social presence, three branded assets, a three-day content plan, a lead-capture preview and a written summary. It closes with a 45-minute call to go through what we found.

Everything produced in the week is yours to keep. If a package or plan fits afterwards we will put it in writing with a fixed scope and price. If nothing fits, we will say so.

Do you take control of my Google or social accounts?

No. We work inside your accounts as an added manager. You own them the whole way through and you can remove our access in two clicks. If an agency asks you to create accounts under their name, that is a reason to walk away.

How fast does any of this actually work?

Profile and listing work shows up in days — hours, photos, booking links and categories update quickly. Search position and review volume take weeks to months, because they depend on real customer behaviour accumulating.

Anyone promising first position in a fortnight is either guessing or selling you something.

Do you only work with hotels?

No — accommodation is one sector out of many, and roughly one client in three. Restaurants, cafés and bars, spas and wellness, dive and watersports, tour and activity operators, golf clubs, clinics treating international visitors, car and bike rental, and real-estate agencies selling to overseas buyers.

The four decision points are the same for all of them: whether a traveller can find you, whether what they see makes them trust you, and whether booking or enquiring takes one tap or five.

Do you write content or run ads?

We write what the profiles, listings and pages we are fixing need. We do not run ads until the basics are right — paying for traffic into a broken booking path is the most common way we see tourism businesses waste money.

What if my business is too small for this?

A single-van tour operator has the same four decision points as a two-hundred-room resort, and usually more to gain, because nobody has ever looked at them. The free week costs you nothing and tells you either way.
