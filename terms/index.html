2What we provide

Marketing work for tourism and hospitality businesses across South-East Asia, in three forms:

  • The free week — a presence check and a sample of the work, at no cost and with nothing to sign
  • Monthly plans — ongoing management, billed monthly
  • One-off packages — a defined piece of work for a one-time fee

What each includes is set out on the plans and packages pages, and confirmed in the quote we send you.

3The free week

The free week is exactly that: free, with no contract and no obligation to continue. Everything we produce during it is yours to keep whether or not you go on to become a client.

We run it once per business. We may decline it where a business is not a fit for what we do, or where we would be working against an existing client in the same market and category.

4Quotes, fees and payment

Prices are quoted per business rather than published, because scope varies. We quote within one working day of your enquiry, and a quote stands for 30 days.

Monthly plans are billed monthly in advance. Packages are billed as set out in the quote. Unless the quote says otherwise, fees exclude third-party costs you would pay anyway — advertising spend, platform subscriptions, domain and hosting fees — and exclude any tax we are required to add.

5Cancelling

Monthly plans can be cancelled at the end of any month. There is no notice period and no cancellation fee. Tell us before your next billing date and the following month is not charged.

Months already paid for are not refunded, and we finish the work for the month you have paid. Packages are one-time work: once we have started, the fee is payable for the work done.

6What we need from you

The work depends on access and answers. You agree to:

  • Give us the access we need to the accounts we are working in
  • Give us accurate information about your business, and tell us when it changes
  • Come back on drafts and approvals within a reasonable time
  • Hold the rights to any photos, logos or copy you give us to use

Where a delay is on your side, timelines move accordingly.

7What you own

Everything we produce for you is yours. Accounts stay in your name, assets stay in your accounts, and nothing is held hostage if you leave. On request we will hand over what we hold and remove our access.

We keep the right to use general skills, methods and know-how developed while working with you, and to describe the work in our own portfolio — unless you ask us not to, which you can do at any time.

8What we cannot promise

This matters, so it is stated plainly. We can improve how a business appears and how easily it converts, and we will show you the change. We cannot promise:

  • A specific position in search results, on Google or anywhere else
  • A specific number of bookings, enquiries, followers or reviews
  • Any particular revenue outcome

Search engines and social platforms rank and display results on their own terms, and those terms change without notice. Anyone who promises you a ranking is either guessing or misleading you.

9Third-party platforms

Much of the work happens on platforms we do not control — Google, Meta, booking sites and review sites. Each has its own rules, and can change them, restrict a feature, or suspend an account. We work within those rules and will tell you when a change affects you, but we are not responsible for a platform's own decisions.
